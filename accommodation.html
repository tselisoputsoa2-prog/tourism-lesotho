Booking tips

Ski chalets and accommodation during popular festival weeks can fill up quickly. Reserve in advance.

Pack warm layers, a rain jacket, a torch, sun protection and cash in maloti or rand. Electricity may be limited at remote camps.

Greet people warmly, ask permission before taking photographs and follow your host's guidance on local customs.
