Four seasons, four countries

Green hills after summer rain, golden grass in autumn, snow-covered peaks in winter and flowers in spring. Pick the Lesotho that suits you.

Pack warm. Highland nights can drop below freezing from May to September, and mountain roads can close after snow.

Summer, December to February

Green hills, summer rain and flowing rivers.

Autumn, March to May

Cooler weather and golden highland landscapes.

Winter, June to August

Snow on the peaks and skiing at Afriski.

Spring, September to November

Waterfalls, wildflowers and fresh mountain scenery.

Why Basotho hospitality makes the trip

Mountains you can reach

Tsehlanyane, Bokong and Sehlabathebe offer trails, rivers and remarkable highland plants.

  • Day hikes from 1 to 6 hours
  • Birdwatching at Bokong
  • Remote camping at Sehlabathebe

A living culture

Thaba Bosiu tells the story of King Moshoeshoe I. Morija offers museums, crafts and cultural festivals.

  • Guided history walks
  • Cave houses at Ha Kome
  • Handmade hats, blankets and baskets

Slow, wide adventure

Ride a pony to a village, visit Maletsunyane Falls or enjoy a boat trip across Katse Dam.

  • Pony trekking from Malealea
  • Skiing in winter
  • Dam and gorge viewpoints

Before you go

Short answers to common visitor questions. Confirm travel rules and prices before travelling.

September to May suits hiking and waterfalls. June to August is suitable for snow and skiing, with cold nights. December to February brings the heaviest rain.

The currency is the loti (plural maloti, written M). The South African rand is also widely accepted. Carry cash for villages and mountain areas.

Minibus taxis connect towns. For the highlands, consider a suitable vehicle or a guided transfer. Avoid mountain passes at night or in snowy conditions.

Visa requirements depend on your passport. Check with the nearest Lesotho embassy or immigration department before booking.
