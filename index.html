Why Visit Lesotho?

Mountains and Nature

Explore Tsehlanyane, Bokong and Sehlabathebe through hiking, birdwatching and camping.

  • Mountain hiking
  • Birdwatching
  • Camping and nature walks

History and Culture

Discover the history of King Moshoeshoe I and experience Basotho traditions.

  • Thaba Bosiu historical site
  • Ha Kome cave houses
  • Morija museums and festivals
  • Traditional hats, blankets and baskets

Adventure

Enjoy outdoor activities and unforgettable experiences in the highlands.

  • Pony trekking at Malealea
  • Winter skiing
  • Boat trips at Katse Dam
  • Waterfalls and mountain viewpoints
