Stay safe in the mountains

  • Check the weather before setting out.
  • Use a local guide on unfamiliar or difficult routes.
  • Tell your host or someone you trust where you are going.
  • Carry enough water, food and warm clothing.
  • Wear suitable shoes and follow local safety advice.
Mountain weather can change quickly. Postpone your activity if conditions are unsafe.
